# Grading Workflows, Policies & Transcripts

Koakademy provides a grading and academic performance engine that balances flexibility for faculty with strict audit compliance and institutional verification.

---

## Supported Grading Systems

Koakademy supports multiple concurrent grading standards configured per program, school, or department (`GradingPolicy` and `GradingPolicyVersion`):

| Grading Scale | Format / Range | Typical Institutional Usage |
| ------------- | -------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Philippine Collegiate Standard** | `1.00` (Highest / Excellent) to `3.00` (Passing), `5.00` (Failed) | State universities, private colleges, CHED degree programs. |
| **Percentage Scale** | `0%` to `100%`, with passing thresholds at `75%` or `60%` | Junior & Senior High Schools (DepEd K-12), preparatory institutions. |
| **US / International Letter Scale** | `A+`, `A`, `A-`, `B+`, `B`, `B-`, `C`, `D`, `F` | International programs, graduate business schools. |
| **Competency / Rubric Standard** | `Competent` (C), `Not Yet Competent` (NYC) | TESDA technical-vocational qualifications. |
| **Qualitative / Pass-Fail** | `P` (Passed), `F` (Failed), `INC` (Incomplete), `DRP` (Dropped) | Thesis defense, physical education, internships, residency. |

## Faculty Grade Encoding & Verification Workflow

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 1. Submission Window│
│    Opens (Schedule) │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           │
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐       [Autosaved Draft]       ┌─────────────────────┐
│ 2. Faculty Portal   │ ────────────────────────────▶ │ Working Draft Sheet │
│    Grade Input      │                               │ (Not Yet Final)     │
└──────────┬──────────┘                               └─────────────────────┘
           │
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 3. Submit & Sign-off│
│    (Review Summary) │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           │
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 4. Dean / Registrar │
│    Verification     │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           │
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 5. Locked & Visible │
│    in Student Portal│
└─────────────────────┘
```

1. **Submission Window Control:** Registrars define opening and closing deadlines for Midterm and Final grade encoding with automatic countdown alerts for faculty.
2. **Draft Autosave:** Faculty can enter grades incrementally. Grades remain in draft status, invisible to students, until formal submission.
3. **Formal Verification:** Once submitted, grades require electronic verification from the Department Chair or College Dean.
4. **Immutable Locking:** Verified grades are locked against tampering. Any subsequent modification requires an authorized, audited **Change of Grade Request** with documented justifications.

## General Weighted Average (GWA) Engine

Koakademy computes students' cumulative and term General Weighted Average in real time:

```txt
GWA = Sum(Final Grade × Credit Units) / Sum(Credit Units)
```

### Configurable Subject Exclusions
Certain curricular subjects should not impact academic honors or cumulative academic standing:
- **National Service Training Program (NSTP / CWTS / ROTC):** Fully recorded for graduation requirements but excluded from collegiate GPA/GWA calculations per CHED regulations.
- **Physical Education (PE):** Optionally excluded or included based on institutional policy.
- **Remedial / Bridging Courses:** Non-credit foundation courses tracked separately from graduation credit totals.

### Academic Honors & Deans List
- **Automated Deans List:** Automatically identifies eligible students per semester who satisfy minimum load requirements (e.g. at least 15 academic units), achieve a minimum GWA (e.g. 1.75 or higher), and have no grade below 2.50 or any Incomplete marks.
- **Latin Honors Calculation:** Evaluates graduating seniors against institutional Latin Honors criteria:
  - *Summa Cum Laude:* GWA 1.00 – 1.20 (no grade below 2.00)
  - *Magna Cum Laude:* GWA 1.21 – 1.45 (no grade below 2.25)
  - *Cum Laude:* GWA 1.46 – 1.75 (no grade below 2.50)

## Official Transcript of Records (TOR) Generation

Koakademy renders official Transcripts of Records directly as high-resolution PDF documents via the Gotenberg engine:

- **Security Watermarking:** Anti-fraud background patterns and institutional seals.
- **Accredited Layout:** Multi-page tabular layout displaying course codes, descriptive titles, final grades, completion terms, and credit units earned.
- **Signatory Blocks:** Automated positioning of Registrar, College Dean, and University President signature blocks.
- **Cryptographic Verification Hash:** A SHA-256 integrity hash is embedded in the document header and rendered as a QR code, allowing third-party employers and foreign universities to verify document authenticity online.
