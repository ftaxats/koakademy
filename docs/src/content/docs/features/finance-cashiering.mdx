# Financial Management, Billing & Cashiering

Koakademy includes an accounting and cashiering subsystem engineered for educational billing. It handles complex fee calculations, installment plans, cashier counter collections, and automated Statement of Account (SOA) generation.

## Fee Structures & Pricing Models

### 1. Tuition Per Unit (`FeeScheduleVersion`)
- **Lecture Units:** Standard classroom instruction rate billed per lecture credit unit.
- **Laboratory Units:** Higher rates reflecting specialized facility consumption (e.g., computer, engineering, culinary, and nursing laboratories).

### 2. Miscellaneous & Auxiliary Fees (`AdditionalFee`)
- **Standard Institutional Fees:** Registration, Library, Athletic, Medical/Dental, Cultural, and Guidance fees.
- **Course-Specific Fees:** Clinical affiliation fees, culinary ingredients fees, computer laboratory maintenance, and graduation fees.

### 3. Discounts & Financial Assistance (`EnrollmentDiscount`)
- **Automated Sibling Discounts:** Sibling detection automatically applies graduated discounts (e.g., 10% off tuition for second child, 20% for third child).
- **Academic Merit Grants:** Top-ranking incoming students receive full or partial tuition waivers tied to maintaining a minimum GWA.
- **Employee Dependents:** Tuition privileges for faculty and administrative staff children.
- **Manual Voucher Posting:** Bursar authorization to post external sponsorship checks (e.g., government tertiary education subsidies, NGO scholarships).

## Installment Payment Plans

Institutions can offer structured payment terms without manual ledger tracking (`StudentTuitionInstallment`):

| Term / Stage | Typical Allocation | Enforcement Gate |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| **Downpayment** | 20% of net tuition | Mandatory for enrollment confirmation and class roster inclusion. |
| **Prelim Exam** | 20% of net tuition | Required for prelim exam permit issuance. |
| **Midterm Exam** | 20% of net tuition | Required for midterm exam permit issuance. |
| **Prefinal Exam** | 20% of net tuition | Required for prefinal exam permit issuance. |
| **Final Exam** | Remaining balance | Required for final exam permit and grade release. |

## Cashier Point-of-Sale (POS) Workflow

The Cashier Module provides a keyboard-optimized counter interface designed for high-throughput payment processing during peak enrollment rushes:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [CASHIER REGISTER]                     Terminal: POS-1│
│                                                        │
│  Student Lookup: [ 2026-00412 ]  JUAN DELA CRUZ        │
│  Program: BS Information Technology                    │
│  Outstanding Balance: ₱14,500.00                       │
│                                                        │
│  Allocations:                                          │
│  [x] Midterm Installment                   ₱5,000.00   │
│  [x] Laboratory Fee Deficiency             ₱1,200.00   │
│                                                        │
│  Total Amount Due:                         ₱6,200.00   │
│  Payment Method: [ Cash / Cheque / Card / GCash ]      │
│  Amount Tendered: ₱7,000.00      Change: ₱800.00       │
│                                                        │
│  [ PRINT OFFICIAL RECEIPT (OR # 2026-9841) ]           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Instant Receipt Generation:** Prints standardized Official Receipts (OR) with automatic breakdown of tuition, VAT (if applicable), and auxiliary fees.
- **Multi-Tender Support:** Accepts split payments across Cash, Cheque, Bank Deposit, and digital payment providers.
- **Daily Drawer Balancing:** End-of-shift reconciliation reporting cash collections, cheques received, and electronic settlements.

## Statements of Account (SOA)

Koakademy generates official Statements of Account on demand for students, parents, and corporate sponsors (`StatementOfAccountIssuance`):

- **Live Balances:** Real-time ledger entries showing every debit assessment, payment receipt, and applied adjustment.
- **Cryptographic Verification:** Each SOA is stamped with a unique verification token and QR code, confirming authenticity when submitted for educational loans or visa applications.
- **Automated Delivery:** Automated scheduled jobs can broadcast electronic statements to registered parent emails prior to upcoming exam periods.
